Prayer Times helps you keep track of your daily prayers and provides useful tools for everyday practice.

Features:
- Prayer times from different calendars and providers
- Offline calculation methods
- Automatic location and worldwide city search
- Adhan playback and prayer notifications
- Automatic silent mode around prayer times
- Early reminders and Makruh time indicator
- Home-screen widgets and ongoing notification
- Qibla compass (2D and map)
- 99 Names of Allah
- Islamic holidays with descriptions
- Dhikr after prayer
- Sahih al-Bukhari hadith collection
- Dhikr counter
- Missed prayer counter
- Downloadable adhan and dua sounds
